# Risk disclosure

**TL;DR:** Digital asset trading carries substantial risk of loss, including total loss of capital. reloadSol is a non-custodial software interface—not a broker, exchange, or financial advisor.

[Return to reloadSol →](https://reloadsol.app/)

---

## Key disclosures

* **Non-custodial software:** reloadSol never takes custody of your assets or private keys. Every live on-chain transaction is signed directly by your wallet.
* **Irreversible transactions:** Blockchain transactions cannot be cancelled, reversed, or refunded once confirmed. Burning a token to reclaim Solana ATA rent permanently destroys that token balance.
* **Market and liquidity risk:** On-chain tokens—especially newly launched or low-liquidity tokens—can lose 90%–100% of their value in seconds. Liquidity pools can be drained without warning.
* **No financial advice:** Nothing in these docs, in the interactive lab (which uses illustrative synthetic examples), or in the reloadSol interface constitutes financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.
* **Spot execution only:** Public reloadSol surfaces provide spot market swaps only—no margin, no leverage, no liquidation, and no exchange-side stop-loss or take-profit orders.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Only trade with funds you can afford to lose completely, and test unfamiliar workflows in [Paper mode](../getting-started/paper-mode.md) before signing live transactions.
{% endhint %}
